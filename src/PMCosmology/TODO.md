# PMCosmology — open work

Repo-wide named premises are listed once in [SCOPE](../../SCOPE.md#named-premises).

The baryon fractions in `Background` inherit the identification of `darkRatio` with cold dark matter per baryon. An independent derivation of that identification would give the composition comparison in `PREDICTIONS.md` more force.

## Retained history and expansion

Does the PM update admit independent inputs at successive ticks, and which later observable can recover them together with the initial state? The capacity bound in `InformationCapacity.retained_history_capacity` assumes such recovery. To connect it to expansion, identify when existing storage is exhausted and derive why retaining the record requires new spatial cells. An expansion rate would also require a rate of record production and a physical volume per cell.

## Physical inputs

1. Specify the expansion history, pressure and energy readout, the scalings of each sector and energy exchange between sectors. Derive how observers infer H₀ and S₈; absolute Hubble units require a clock.
2. Specify the primordial source or initial state that fixes mode amplitudes and power. A spectral tilt needs a spectrum.
3. Derive a baryon-changing event, its initialization and dilution, and resolve the unequal CP prescriptions. A neutrino floor depends on a nonuniform propagation law and particle spectrum.
4. Determine the response selecting the metric readout, spatial metric, field equation and global causal structure.
5. Fix cell area and entropy normalization, and distinguish total from inactive substrate counts and their occupations before using them as abundances.
6. With background, perturbations and observational readout specified, compare their joint likelihood. H₀ and S₈ rescalings share one load parameter.
