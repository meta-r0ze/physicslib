- **Electroweak scale (`v`, `G_F`).** Why the boundary's `χ` closures act in parallel around a width-`D` boundary. The boundary admittance is built from the same three facts as `Z_geo` (Barbier boundary length, Descartes closures, Ohm), and the Fermi window excludes every sibling in that construction: series closures, width `Δ`, one closure, `σ` or `ν` in place of `χ`, no boundary length (`VevBoundary`). At `D = 4` it cannot be told apart from `χ` over the full solid angle `4π`. The linear form, the series-impedance structure and the margin's one-axis share are derived (`VevImpedance`, `VevReadout`, `VevEvent`).
