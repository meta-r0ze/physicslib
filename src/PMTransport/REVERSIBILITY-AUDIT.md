# Reversibility audit

The system's retained update and the exported record are different
parts of the operation. The substrate paper's three-leg argument
uses Conservation for the local leg update. The PM paper gives
Landauer's bound for erasure of an unbiased bit in its standard
thermal setting. The boundary account uses one Toll unit for one
designated binary erasure. It supplies neither a physical erasure
event nor a tariff for other operations.

Classes below are **(a)** retained internal result, **(b)** boundary
claim whose scope must be stated, and **(c)** count or selection on
a family that retains the listed registers. Grouped entries cover
the trace, inverse and counting consequences of the stated update.
