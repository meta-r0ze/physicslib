**Substrate premise** marks a supplied parameter or identification still to derive from the E8 update. **Certificate** marks an external calculation awaiting formal verification. **Route premise** marks a requirement of a particular derivation route. **Extension** marks a stronger result beyond the present theorem scope. ## Substrate premises

- **Node-restoring amplitude: occupied legs (substrate premise).** `ReferenceClassHop` sends the two light insertions (and two of the three transfers) through a reference-restoring process; `ReferenceNode` models it as a conserving node with one leg price and legs only to occupied carrier modes, and derives `k/K = α/2` (every restoring move involves exactly two classes, `restoring_two_classes`). Derive the occupied-legs premise (a node with legs to all three classes would give `α/3`, `star_three`), and that the reference occupies one class (used by `ReferenceClassHop`, `ReferenceRouting`, `OrientationDoublet`). Checked (`tools/reference_class_check.py`): with each native class exchange either kept (the common scalar of `uniform_hop`) or suppressed, the stationary references are a single class with the opposite exchange kept (`ReferenceClassHop`), or an antisymmetric two-class combination `(e_s − e_c)/√2`-type with the two exchanges into the third class kept, or anything with no exchange; keeping all three leaves none. The two-class family gives two direct insertions (two heavy generations) and makes the transfer through the reference's class the node one, both against the data's one heavy generation and large Cabibbo angle; it is also not a single carrier, while the library's reference is one (`PMConstants.CarrierRepresentation`'s `+2e₀`). So the single class is the carrier reference; deriving that the reference is one carrier (rather than a two-class superposition) is the remaining step. (A short attempt: neither the global sign under the `s ↔ c` reflection nor the equal-class crossing separates the two-class reference from a carrier; the argument has to come from how the reference's maintenance records it, `NativeReferenceBackground`: one common reference per payload label.) At the resonance with the other wall class-blind, `m₂/m₃ ≈ √(3/2) k/K`; against data `k` is nearly degenerate with the EP heavy-label weight, so it is fixed by the derivation, not by fitting. The leave/enter asymmetry of the node CKM edges is not from the node (PMFlavor REVIEW, "Leg count"). Dependents in PMAction: `NodeAmplitude`, `GenerationMass`, `MassInsertion`, `LightSplit`, `GenerationCurrent`, `WallHopPhase`.
- **Event clock: crossing, carrier and flip rates (substrate premise).** Derive the duration of one native event in hop-time units and the substrate tick, then the wall-crossing rate `y` and the in-wall carrier and flip rates in those units (a certain event of duration `τ` has rate `≥ π/(2τ)`, `CertainTransferRate`; turning a node admittance into a Hamiltonian rate needs the same duration). `WallGapCalibration`: the native realization, glue support and stationary reference alone admit every flip-to-crossing ratio, so a microscopic relation is needed. `SplitClockBoundary` excludes identifying the split update with one Hamiltonian exponential; compare the finite-tick update with the wall evolution on the light subspace instead. At the derived rates, certify the light-band error with `LightLeakage`.
- **Lazy or coherent flip event (substrate premise).** Economy fixes the other wall's flip on a root of `δ³ = −κ_O0κ_O1κ_O2` (`OtherWallStationary`). As the lazy `β` event it is the real root, realized by attempt probability `(1 − e^{−|δ|})/2` (`OtherWallFlipEvent`), with a wall-local offset that turns the light masses into `s²/c'` (`WallOffset`); as a coherent event it keeps the singular values `s` but needs its identification and duration. Derive which the native event is (the same clock question as above).
- **Colour register (substrate premise).** `ColourRegister`: colour that commutes with `β` and the `αⱼ` cannot act on the carrier axes, so it is a separate factor. Construct that factor natively (which E8 structure carries the three colours). `NativeDetuning`'s per-protocol crossing paragraph reads colour as axes and needs re-deriving with it. Candidates, cheaply tested (`tools/register_candidates_check.py`). Tests: (T1) light content `32` components per generation; (T2) a `1 + 3` split of the label under the stabilizer of the reference carrier `+2e₀`; (T3) a weak-doublet sign; (T4) the label stationary under the wall (`EvenComplement`); (T5) a native E8 origin. A generation is a carrier pair `8_b ⊗ 8_c`, one per wall; its mixed part `odd ⊗ even ⊕ even ⊗ odd` is the two-wall Dirac space times a 4-valued even label of the other wall, on which the wall acts as `H ⊗ 1`.
  * *Killed:* the other wall's even modes as the label (T1: `2 × 4 × 2 = 16`; T2: the stabilizer is transitive on the spinor classes' four even labels, orbit sizes `[4]`, so the label-`v` generation, whose classes are `s` and `c`, has no `1 + 3`); the other wall's full eight carriers (T4: the odd half is the wall); chiral labels (`e_c` for one wall, `e_b` for the other: T1 `16`); the two zero modes per wall as the doublet (they are spin).
  * *Survives (a):* the label is the even sector of the reference's own class `v` (orbits `[1, 3]`: T2, T4), and the doublet is the generation's two wall orientations `(b, c)` and `(c, b)`. Their exchange is a root move by the generation's label `m` and is the only root move that keeps both classes nontrivial (T5, `a = m` in every generation), so every generation's partner exchange is one norm-two root. T1: `2 × 16 = 32`. Open: where the reference class's even sector sits as a factor of each generation's state, and a test against data: on the wire the label-`v` generation's two orientations are related by the `s ↔ c` swap, which a colour-axis reflection realizes while fixing the reference (PMElementaryParticles `DoubletReflection.flip_spinor`), and so degenerate, so its partner splitting (`m_t/m_b ≈ 41`) must come from the protocol's charge weight. Tested on the derived wire: the two orientations (cycle `P` versus `Pᵀ`) have the same singular values at every root and weight, so all partner splitting is the charge weight (consistent with the fitted `s_u ≠ s_d`), and the orientation adds no mixing (with up and down in opposite orientations the CKM is the same as before). Support (PMFlavor `OrientationDoublet`): every elementary move reverses the orientation (single-wall shifts change the generation, the root move by the label keeps it), so with the orientation as the doublet every move is an up ↔ down transition, the generation-changing ones routed direct or through the node by `ReferenceRouting`: the charged current's pattern, with no single-move neutral flavour change.
  * *Survives (b):* the library's present route, the reference class's eight carriers as an occupation register (one occupied carrier: four axes `×` doublet sign, `CarrierRepresentation`, `IndependentRegisters`), two hands from the walls: passes T1–T4 by construction; T5 needs the bank's E8 origin.
