# PMAction — open work

Checked results are in [OVERVIEW](OVERVIEW.md).

**Substrate premise** marks a supplied parameter or identification still to derive from the E8 update. **Certificate** marks an external calculation awaiting formal verification. **Route premise** marks a requirement of a particular derivation route. **Extension** marks a stronger result beyond the present theorem scope. Each current theorem states its hypotheses.

## Substrate premises

- **Node-restoring amplitude `k/K` (substrate premise).** `ReferenceClassHop` derives the split on the wire: the reference's stationarity forbids direct exchange into or out of its class on its wall, so only the label-`v` generation has a direct insertion (`κ_R = (K, 0, 0)`), and the other two need a process that restores the reference. Derive that process and its amplitude `k/K` (`NodeAmplitude`'s two legs of `α` give `α/2`; one leg gives `α`); at the resonance with the other wall class-blind, `m₂/m₃ ≈ √(3/2) k/K`. Which node structure applies is not fixed yet: a series of two legs `α` gives `α/2`; a star node joined to all three classes gives `α/3` per pair; a class-`v` mode that sits on the node needs one leg (`α` as a probability or amplitude). Against data `k` is nearly degenerate with the EP heavy-label weight `s` (the fitted `s` scales with `k`, so the `s` ratios between protocols barely change), so derive it together with that weight. Also derive that the reference occupies one class (used as a premise, the vector class). Dependents in PMAction: `NodeAmplitude`, `GenerationMass`, `MassInsertion`, `LightSplit`, `GenerationCurrent`, `WallHopPhase`.
- **Event clock: crossing, carrier and flip rates (substrate premise).** Derive the duration of one native event in hop-time units and the substrate tick, then the wall-crossing rate `y` and the in-wall carrier and flip rates in those units (a certain event of duration `τ` has rate `≥ π/(2τ)`, `CertainTransferRate`; turning a node admittance into a Hamiltonian rate needs the same duration). `WallGapCalibration`: the native realization, glue support and stationary reference alone admit every flip-to-crossing ratio, so a microscopic relation is needed. `SplitClockBoundary` excludes identifying the split update with one Hamiltonian exponential; compare the finite-tick update with the wall evolution on the light subspace instead. At the derived rates, certify the light-band error with `LightLeakage`.
- **Lazy or coherent flip event (substrate premise).** Economy fixes the other wall's flip on a root of `δ³ = −κ_O0κ_O1κ_O2` (`OtherWallStationary`). As the lazy `β` event it is the real root, realized by attempt probability `(1 − e^{−|δ|})/2` (`OtherWallFlipEvent`), with a wall-local offset that turns the light masses into `s²/c'` (`WallOffset`); as a coherent event it keeps the singular values `s` but needs its identification and duration. Derive which the native event is (the same clock question as above).
- **Colour register (substrate premise).** `ColourRegister`: colour that commutes with `β` and the `αⱼ` cannot act on the carrier axes, so it is a separate factor. Construct that factor natively (which E8 structure carries the three colours). `NativeDetuning`'s per-protocol crossing paragraph reads colour as axes and needs re-deriving with it. Candidate (not yet formalized): a two-wall state is a pair of carriers, one per `D₄` sector (`GenerationLabel`: generation `g` puts class `g + 1` on one wall and `g − 1` on the other), so its amplitude space is the tensor product `8_b ⊗ 8_c` of the two walls' carrier spaces. Each factor splits into odd (Dirac, 4) and even (4), and the even part is stationary under the wall (`EvenComplement`). The mixed part `odd ⊗ even ⊕ even ⊗ odd` is then the wire's two-wall Dirac space with a 4-valued label carried by the *other* wall's even modes, and the wall dynamics acts on it as `H ⊗ 1`, so a colour action on the label commutes with every wall term without living on the axes (`ColourRegister`). For the vector class the label is one function per axis, one plus three about the reference's axis. The wall crossing then has to move the label with the Dirac part (a slot exchange, as in `WallExchange`'s swap with parity). Count: the light content of the mixed part is `2` zero modes per wall × `4` labels × `2` walls `= 16` components per generation, half of a generation's `32`: the weak doublet is not located by this construction. Formalize the pairing and find the doublet factor (the generation's second class, or the antipode sign, `DoubletSign.weak_partner_is_antipode`).
